About CareerPath

CareerPath is a student-oriented strand exploration portal designed to provide Grade 10 students with accessible information about Senior High School pathways, subjects, possible college courses, and career opportunities.

Purpose of the Website

The website aims to help students become more informed about their educational choices by presenting strand-related information in an organized and accessible format.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Senior High School strand?

A strand is an educational pathway that allows students to focus on learning areas connected to their interests and goals.


2. Does the assessment decide my future strand?

No. It only provides a suggested area to explore. The final decision should also consider your interests, goals, and guidance from teachers and family.


3. Can I take a college course that is different from my strand?

College admission requirements vary by institution and program. Check the requirements of the college you plan to attend.


4. Where can I confirm the strands offered by MNHS?

Ask the school's guidance office or official school representatives for the current list of available programs.

Important: This website is an educational prototype. Its strand descriptions and examples should be reviewed and updated based on the school's official information before actual use.
